How are Unley Park, Mitcham, Blackwood etc. estimated? ~
The ARTC plan only timestamps freight at Keswick and Belair on the suburban Belair line. For stations between them (shown with a ~), times are estimated from each train's own Keswick↔Belair journey time, distributed across four terrain zones:
ZoneKm from KeswickSpeed
Keswick approach  accel/decel from stop0.0–0.7~25 km/h
Suburban cruise  Keswick → Mitcham0.7–4.7~45 km/h
Climb  Mitcham → Blackwood, 1-in-45 grade4.7–14.3~30 km/h
Plateau cruise  Blackwood → Belair14.3–17.7~65 km/h
Distances measured from Keswick (the freight terminal). Add 3.8 km for the official ARTC kilometrage from Adelaide railway station.
Validated against 57 scheduled paths; agrees with the observed median journey time of ~29 min. Accurate to about ±1–2 min for an unhindered train. Belair isn't modelled as a slowdown — through freight stays on the mainline at line speed. Direction (uphill vs downhill) only shifts speeds ~10 % here: power going up and brake heat coming down constrain trains similarly.
